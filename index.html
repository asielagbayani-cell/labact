About us

UST College of Information and Computing Sciences (CICS) provides quality education in technology and computing. It develops students' technical skills, creativity, and problem-solving abilities, preparing future IT professionals through learning and innovation.

Our Mission and Vision

🎯

Mission

The Institute of Information and Computing Sciences of the University of Santo Tomas, under the inspiration of its model, Saint Thomas Aquinas, and its patron, Saint Vincent Ferrer, declares its commitment to the pursuit of truth and the formation of technically competent professionals endowed with a strong commitment to social responsibility and a desire for leadership in the spirit of service in their fields of specialization.

👁️

Vision

The Institute of Information and Computing Sciences envisions itself as a premier and innovative institution in Information Technology Education and research in the Asia-Pacific Region.

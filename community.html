The CICS Community

The College of Information and Computing Sciences fosters a vibrant community of students, faculty, and student organizations, dedicated to leadership, collaboration, and innovation in technology. Alongside their academic duties, the faculty provide expertise, mentorship, and guidance, ensuring students grow both academically and professionally. Together, they create a dynamic environment that supports learning, research, and service within the fields of computer science, information systems, and information technology.

STUDENT ORGANIZATIONS

CNAG-CICS

A student organization focused on advancing Cisco expertise and inspiring innovation, creativity, and passion. CNAG-CICS strengthens practical skills, reinforces classroom learning, and equips young IT professionals for real-world network security challenges through workshops, reviews, competitions, and hands-on experiences.

Computer Science Society

A recognized student group at the University of Santo Tomas, composed of dedicated Computer Science students. It strives for academic excellence, fosters a compassionate community guided by Church teachings, and enhances members' knowledge through activities, seminars, and training that promote technological innovation.

Society of Information Technology Enthusiasts (SITE)

The central organization of the IT Department at CICS, committed to shaping students into distinguished IT professionals. It builds a supportive community that develops technical skills and nurtures compassionate, competent, and committed practitioners in the field.

Information Systems Society

The leading student-led group for the Information Systems program at CICS. With a mission to drive purposeful technology, ISS cultivates technical expertise, leadership, and professional values, preparing members to thrive as competent and compassionate IS professionals in a dynamic tech environment.

Pax Romana - CICS

The sole religious organization within CICS, fostering reflection, transformation, and solidarity. Founded in 1941, it advocates peace and holistic formation, guiding Catholic students to become compassionate professionals dedicated to serving the Church, nation, and global community while promoting social change.

PATRON SAINT

St. Vincent Ferrer

Feast Day: May 5 (1350-1419), priest

St. Vincent Ferrer dedicated his life to guiding people toward repentance and spiritual renewal. Known for his extraordinary gifts of preaching, prophecy, and speaking in tongues, he became a powerful messenger of faith and preparation for divine judgment. As the patron of the UST Institute of Information and Computing Sciences, St. Vincent Ferrer inspires the college's mission to pursue truth and excellence. His example encourages the formation of technically skilled IT professionals who embody lifelong learning, social responsibility, and leadership rooted in service.
